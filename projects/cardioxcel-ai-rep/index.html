Numbers start from a fictional pilot. Questions you ask on the call are added live.

1. Approved facts onlyEvery answer comes from a fixed library of approved product information, and shows its source. The AI never makes up an answer.
2. Always honest it's AIThe avatar says it's an AI at the start of every call and is labelled on screen.
3. Human hand-offOff-label questions (pregnancy, children, higher doses) and anything not in the library go straight to Medical Information.
4. Safety reportingIf a doctor mentions a side effect, it is flagged for the safety team (pharmacovigilance).
5. Questions become insightEvery question is anonymised and grouped into themes on the brand team's dashboard.
6. Test and learnTwo versions of content are tested, and the one doctors actually use wins.
