My concept, and I led the team of four. TRYOUT was a virtual fitting plugin for clothing e-commerce sites, built to answer the question that stops people buying clothes online: how will this actually look on me.

The pitch was structured the way I still approach problems now. Define the customer problem, quantify its impact on the business, size the market against a cited source, then design the commercial model. In this case a licensing model selling software rights to e-commerce platforms.
