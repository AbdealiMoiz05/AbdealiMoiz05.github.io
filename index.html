Case study

Cutting order-processing delays by 20%

Decent Balloons, a party and events retailer running roughly 200 orders a week across four sales channels.

Result: 20% reduction in processing delay
The problem

Orders were arriving through four separate channels and being handled inconsistently. Nobody could say where an order actually sat at any given moment, and delays were being treated as a staffing problem rather than a process one.

What I did

I mapped the workflow end to end, from first enquiry through to despatch, and traced where orders were sitting idle. Once the bottleneck was visible I redesigned the handoffs around it, wrote the new steps up as standard operating procedures, and trained the team on them so the change held after I stopped watching it.

The outcome

Processing delays fell by 20%. Order handling became consistent across all four channels, and the SOPs meant new staff could be brought up to speed without shadowing someone for a week.

What I took from it

The fix was not more people or better software. It was making the existing process visible enough that the constraint became obvious. That is the part of the work I want to do professionally.
